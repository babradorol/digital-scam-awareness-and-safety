📚 Understand the Scams

Knowledge is your first line of defence. Learn how scammers trick people and what warning signs to look for.

🎣

Phishing Scams

Scammers send fake emails, texts or messages pretending to be banks, companies or trusted organisations to steal passwords, PINs or personal details.

Warning signs: Urgent threats, strange links or requests for secret information.

Stay safe: Visit the official website or app yourself instead of clicking suspicious links.

💼

Fake Job Offers

Fraudsters advertise attractive jobs but may ask applicants to pay registration, training or interview fees before getting hired.

Warning signs: Guaranteed employment, pressure to pay quickly or vague company details.

Stay safe: Verify the employer using official contact details before sharing documents or money.

🛍️

Online Shopping Scams

Fake sellers advertise products at tempting prices, collect payment and then fail to deliver the goods.

Warning signs: Prices that seem too good to be true, copied photos or pressure to pay immediately.

Stay safe: Check seller reviews and use payment methods with appropriate buyer protection.

📈

Investment Scams

Scammers promise unusually high or guaranteed profits to persuade people to send money into fake investment schemes.

Warning signs: Guaranteed returns, secret opportunities or rewards for recruiting others.

Stay safe: Independently verify the business and its regulatory status before investing.

🎭

Impersonation Scams

Someone pretends to be a friend, relative, official or customer-care agent and asks for money or confidential information.

Warning signs: Unexpected emergencies, unusual payment requests or requests for a one-time code.

Stay safe: Contact the person through a separate, trusted channel to confirm their identity.

📱

Account Takeover Scams

Criminals try to access email, social media or financial accounts using stolen passwords or verification codes.

Warning signs: Unexpected login alerts or requests to share a verification code.

Stay safe: Never share login codes, use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

💜 Remember

Anyone can encounter a scam. If something feels suspicious, pause, verify the information independently and avoid sending money or personal details until you are sure.
