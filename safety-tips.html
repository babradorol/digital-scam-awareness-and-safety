💡 Your Daily Safety Tips

Stay alert, protect your information and make safer choices online. Start with these simple habits.

🔍

1. Verify Before You Trust

Confirm unexpected messages, job offers and payment requests through an independent, trusted source.

🔐

2. Keep Your PINs and Passwords Secret

Never disclose your banking PIN, password or one-time verification code to someone who requests it.

⏳

3. Don't Give In to Pressure

Scammers may create urgency. Take time to check the facts before sending money or sharing information.

🛒

4. Research Online Sellers

Check independent reviews, seller history and payment protections before buying from an unfamiliar seller.

💼

5. Check Job Opportunities

Verify the company and vacancy using official channels. Be cautious if payment is demanded to secure a job.

📈

6. Question Guaranteed Profits

Every investment carries risk. Independently verify investment providers and be wary of promises of unusually high guaranteed returns.

📱

7. Update Your Devices

Install security updates and use a screen lock to help protect your devices and accounts.

🚨

8. Act Quickly if You Suspect a Scam

Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if money or financial details may be at risk. Preserve evidence and report the incident through appropriate official channels.
