🛡️ Your Online Safety Guide

Small security habits can make a big difference. Learn how to protect yourself whenever you use the internet.

🔑

Protect Your Passwords

Use strong, unique passwords for different accounts. Consider a trusted password manager and enable two-factor authentication where available.

🔗

Check Before Clicking

Inspect unexpected links and attachments carefully. Open the official website or app directly when a message asks you to sign in or make a payment.

🤫

Keep Information Private

Never share passwords, PINs or one-time verification codes. Share identity documents and personal details only when necessary with verified recipients.

💳

Protect Your Money

Confirm payment requests independently. Check recipient details before sending money, and never let someone rush you into a financial decision.

📲

Secure Your Devices

Keep your phone, computer, browser and applications updated. Use a screen lock and install applications from trusted sources.

🌐

Browse Carefully

Be careful when using public Wi-Fi. Avoid accessing sensitive accounts on untrusted networks, and never ignore browser security warnings.

💜 Before You Trust a Message

  • Who sent it, and can I verify their identity?
  • Is the message pressuring me to act immediately?
  • Is it asking for money, a password or a verification code?
  • Can I confirm the claim using an independent official source?

If you're unsure, pause and verify first.
