🚨 How to Report a Scam

Suspect that you have encountered a scam? These steps can help you respond safely and report what happened.

1

Stop and Protect Yourself

Do not send more money, click suspicious links, or share passwords, PINs or verification codes. Stop communicating with the suspected scammer if it is safe to do so.

2

Keep the Evidence

Save relevant messages, screenshots, phone numbers, email addresses, website links, transaction references and dates. Do not put yourself at risk to collect evidence.

3

Contact Your Bank or Payment Provider

If you sent money or shared financial information, contact your bank, mobile money provider or payment service promptly through its official channels. Ask what protective steps are available.

4

Secure Your Accounts

If you shared a password, change it using the official website or app. Enable two-factor authentication where available, and sign out unknown sessions if possible.

5

Report Through an Appropriate Official Channel

Report the incident to the relevant platform and, where appropriate, the police or the relevant cybercrime reporting authority. Use independently verified official contact details and keep any reference number you receive.

6

Submit a Report on ScamSafe

You can document the suspected incident on ScamSafe so that your submission can be tracked within the system. ScamSafe is an awareness and reporting-information system; submitting a report here does not automatically notify the police, your bank or the platform.

⚠️ Important Safety Reminder

Never pay someone who promises to recover lost money in exchange for an upfront fee without independently verifying the service. Be cautious of people who claim they can guarantee recovery. Never share your PIN, password or one-time verification code.
